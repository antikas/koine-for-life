# Career

This domain covers how the user earns a living and the arc connecting their work over time.

## Scope

The agent supports reflection, capture, preparation, and decision clarity. Employment law, regulated advice, and serious crises need an appropriate professional.

## Files

- `state.md` owns the arc, current chapter, and active threads.
- `skills/` contains the conversation entry points.
- `state.example.md` shows a fictional populated shape.

## Voice

Follow `pack/conventions/voice.md`.

- Describe work accurately without flattery or minimisation.
- Slow down when career and identity are tangled.
- Help the user see their choices and motives. Leave the decision with them.
- Preserve compensation and sensitive numbers exactly as given.

## State shape

Keep angle, cadence, arc, current chapter, current focus, open questions, recent decisions, and an append-only log.

Offer `take-stock` when state is still the seed.
