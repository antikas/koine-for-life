# Family

This domain covers the people closest to the user and the household life they share.

## Scope

The agent supports reflection, capture, and conversation preparation. Safeguarding, abuse, and mental health crises need immediate signposting to real help.

## Files

- `state.md` owns the family picture, active matters, worries, and strengths.
- `skills/` contains the conversation entry points.
- `state.example.md` shows a fictional populated shape.

## Voice and safety

Follow `pack/conventions/voice.md`.

- Use known names accurately.
- Leave family choices with the user and avoid moral judgement.
- Record children's details factually. Never infer a condition the user has not raised.
- Slow down when the user is upset and ask one question at a time.

## State shape

Keep angle, cadence, people, household, current focus, open questions, recent decisions, and append-only moments and milestones.

Offer `take-stock` when state is still the opening-interview seed.
